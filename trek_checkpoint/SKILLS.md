# SKILLS.md — Trek Checkpoint SL 6 AXS Gen 2 maintenance skills

A running index of the mechanic skills relevant to this bike. Each entry: what it is,
when to do it, the bike-specific gotchas, and tools needed. Check items off as you
learn/do them. See `specs.md` for parts and torque values.

Status key: ☐ not done · ◐ done once, need practice · ☑ confident

---

## 1. Tubeless tire setup & repair
Status: ☐

- Both wheelsets (Paradigm Comp 25 700c + the 650b set) are tubeless-ready.
- Skills: seat a tubeless tire, add sealant through the valve core, plug a trail
  cut, boot a large gash, refit a tube when tubeless fails on a ride.
- Gotchas: 25 mm inner-ish rim — GR1 40c sits fine; going wider (45c 700c / 50c+
  650b) changes clearance to the chainstay/fork. Refresh sealant every 2–4 months.
- If these lose air faster than you'd like, the usual fixes are fresh rim tape, a
  check of the valve-stem seal/lockring, and making sure the tire bead is fully seated.
- Tools: tubeless floor pump or booster/CO2, valve core tool, sealant, plugs,
  tire boot, 2 tire levers, spare tube (700x35–45 covers both wheelsets in a pinch).

## 2. SRAM AXS — batteries, pairing, firmware
Status: ☐

- Three AXS batteries on this bike: rear derailleur, **Reverb AXS XPLR dropper**,
  plus the coin cells in each shifter/pod. Derailleur and dropper batteries are
  interchangeable — carry one spare.
- Skills: swap/charge batteries, hard reset a component (function button), re-pair
  a component to the system, check/upgrade firmware and battery health in the SRAM
  AXS app, adjust rear shift micro-adjust (12 clicks) from the app or the buttons.
- Gotchas: when a battery gets low the **dropper is deprioritized** before the
  derailleur. Keep the derailleur battery as the "known good" one. Cold weather
  drops range noticeably.
- Tools: AXS charger, phone with AXS app, small flat screwdriver.

## 3. Rear derailleur setup (Rival XPLR eTap AXS)
Status: ☐

- Skills: set high/low limit screws, set chain gap with the SRAM chain-gap gauge
  (or credit-card method), index via micro-adjust, hanger alignment.
- Gotchas: XPLR long cage + 10–44 cassette — chain gap matters for shift quality
  and clutch behaviour. Use the correct SRAM Flattop chain and Flattop-specific
  quick link (single-use). Chain length: wrap around 40T/44T + 2 links, no RD.
- Tools: chain-gap gauge, hanger alignment gauge, Flattop chain breaker,
  quick-link pliers, spare Flattop quick links, T25.

## 4. Hydraulic disc brakes — SRAM Rival (DOT fluid)
Status: ☐

- Skills: pad swap, piston reset/clean, rotor truing, bed-in new pads, Bleeding
  Edge bleed (lever + caliper), lever reach + contact-point (bite) adjust.
- Gotchas: SRAM = **DOT 5.1 fluid** (not mineral oil — never mix). Flat-mount
  calipers; know the front adapter for 160 mm. Keep DOT off paint, pads, rotors.
- Tools: SRAM Bleed Kit (Bleeding Edge), DOT 5.1, T10/T25, pad spreader,
  isopropyl alcohol, new pads (organic vs metallic — organic quieter, metallic
  lasts in grit).

## 5. RockShox Reverb AXS XPLR dropper service
Status: ☐

- Skills: set/verify it's fully topped (it's air-sprung — the XPLR uses an air
  return), clean the stanchion + wiper after muddy rides, check for sag/squish,
  send for lowers service / full rebuild per SRAM interval.
- Gotchas: 27.2 mm post in a 27.2 mm frame — minimal wall, keep the seat tube
  clean and lightly greased/carbon-paste per Trek. 75 mm travel only; don't
  force past the stop. Actuation is wireless — no cable/hose to route.
- Tools: shock pump (for the XPLR's air side, if serviced), isopropyl, grease,
  SRAM service kit at interval.

## 6. T47 threaded bottom bracket (DUB Wide)
Status: ☐ — **active issue:** load creak at 3/9 o'clock after a shop BB swap. Full diagnosis procedure in `MOP-bb-creak-diagnosis.md` / `.html`, assembly diagram in `diagrams/sram-dub-crank-t47-bb.svg`.

- Skills: remove/install T47 cups, check for creak, re-grease threads, swap the
  DUB bearing preload/wave washer, diagnose DUB spindle play.
- Gotchas: **T47 internal-bearing** shell, threaded (easy compared to press-fit).
  This crank is **DUB Wide** — the BB must be DUB Wide-compatible; chainline
  ~47.5 mm. Torque 35–45 Nm. Grease threads to kill creak.
- Tools: T47 BB tool (16-notch, 44 mm — confirm which the installed brand needs),
  torque wrench to 45 Nm, DUB grease, 8 mm hex for the crank bolt.

## 7. IsoSpeed decoupler
Status: ☐

- Skills: inspect for play/creak, check the pivot bolt torque, grease/replace the
  cartridge bearings at service interval, silence a creaking decoupler.
- Gotchas: Gen 2 rear IsoSpeed is **non-adjustable** (unlike SLR/Domane sliders).
  Most issues are dry bearings or a loose bolt — creak often mistaken for BB or
  seatpost. Service per the Checkpoint SL service manual.
- Tools: torque wrench, hex/Torx set, replacement IsoSpeed bearing kit, grease.

## 8. Internal cable/hose routing & headset
Status: ☐

- Skills: route the rear brake hose through the frame (guided ports on Gen 2 —
  not fully through-headset), bleed after routing, headset bearing service, set
  headset preload, deal with the compression ring/top cap.
- Gotchas: AXS means **only one hose** (rear brake) runs internally — front is
  external at the fork. Much simpler than a Domane. Keep headset bearings greased;
  grit from gravel kills the lower bearing.
- Tools: hex set, grease, cable/hose port grommets, headset bearing press if
  replacing races.

## 9. Downtube storage (BITS) hatch
Status: ☐

- Skills: operate the latch, keep the seal clean/lubed, use the BITS tube/bags,
  check the hatch gasket for water intrusion.
- Gotchas: don't overstuff — rattles and can foul the bottle bolts. Silicone
  grease on the gasket. A rattle here is often blamed on the frame.
- Tools: silicone grease, the Trek BITS storage bags.

## 10. Wheel swap workflow (700c ↔ 650b)
Status: ☐

- Skills: quick swap procedure — thru-axles, rotor check, brake pad clearance,
  re-index if cassette spacing differs, adjust dropper/saddle height for the
  ~15–20 mm BB height change, tire pressure per wheel/tire.
- Gotchas: 650b drops the BB and slackens the bike slightly; watch pedal strike.
  Confirm both wheelsets use the same rotor size (160/160) or the caliper needs
  re-centering. Chainline with DUB Wide is aimed at exactly this dual-setup.
- Tools: thru-axle handle/6 mm hex, torque wrench, pressure gauge.

## 11. Drivetrain wear & cleaning
Status: ☐

- Skills: chain wear check (0.5 / 0.75 gauge — Flattop needs a **Flattop-specific**
  gauge), degrease + relube for gravel/wet, cassette + chainring wear, clean the
  RD pulleys.
- Gotchas: Flattop chains and 12s XPLR parts wear as a system — replace chain
  early (~0.5%) to save the 10–44 cassette (expensive, XDR). Wax or a good wet
  lube for gravel dust.
- Tools: Flattop chain checker, chain keeper/quick link, degreaser, lube, brush set.

## 12. Contact-point fit & cockpit
Status: ☐

- Skills: set saddle height/setback/tilt (now with a dropper — measure to full
  extension), bar roll + reach with the 48 cm flared bar, re-wrap tape, brake
  lever angle for the hoods.
- Gotchas: 48 cm bar is wider than stock — more leverage/control off-road but
  watch shoulder comfort and aero on road. Flare means the drops are wider than
  the hoods; set hood angle first.
- Tools: 4/5 mm hex, torque wrench (5 Nm cockpit), bar tape, fit notes in specs.md.

## Recurring maintenance calendar (adjust to mileage)

| Interval | Task |
|---|---|
| Every ride | Tire pressure, quick brake + shift check, wipe chain |
| Weekly / post-gravel | Clean drivetrain, relube, check for play, wipe dropper stanchion |
| Monthly | Chain wear gauge, brake pad check, bolt-torque spot check, tubeless top-up |
| ~3 months | Full sealant refresh, headset + IsoSpeed inspection, AXS firmware/battery health |
| ~6 months / 1 yr | Brake bleed, BB service/inspect, dropper lowers service, cable/bearing service |
| Annually | Full strip, frame inspection (carbon armor, storage gasket), fork lower/bearing service |
