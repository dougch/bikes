## This build (changes from stock)

| Item | Stock | This bike |
|---|---|---|
| Crank | SRAM Rival, DUB, 40T | SRAM Rival **40T, DUB Wide**, 172.5 mm arms |
| Seatpost | Bontrager rigid carbon, 27.2 mm | **RockShox Reverb AXS XPLR dropper, 75 mm travel, 27.2 mm** (wireless) |
| Handlebar | Bontrager alloy gravel bar, ~44 cm (on 54 cm frame) | **48 cm wide** bar |
| Pedals | none / basic | **Time ATAC** (clipless) |
| Wheels | Bontrager Paradigm Comp 25 (700c) | Keeps **OEM 700c** set + a **650b** set |

Notes:
- **DUB Wide** = wider spindle / ~47.5 mm chainline. Pairs well with 650b and fat 700c tires; needs a DUB Wide-compatible BB (T47, wide).
- Reverb AXS XPLR is 27.2 mm — matches the frame's 27.2 mm seat tube. Its own AXS battery (same as the derailleur battery), controlled by a left-hand AXS pod/blip.
- Two wheelsets means keeping two cassettes (both XDR driver, 10-44 XPLR) and swapping rotors/tires per set. Confirm both wheelsets are 12x100 front / 12x142 rear thru-axle.

## Stock specification (SL 6 AXS Gen 2, 2023)

### Frame & fork
- **Frame:** 500 Series OCLV Carbon; rear **IsoSpeed** decoupler; carbon "armor" on downtube; internal cable routing; **downtube storage** (BITS hatch); 3S chain keeper; **T47 threaded BB**; flat-mount disc; rack + fender + top-tube/frame-bag mounts; dropper-post compatible; **12x142 mm** rear thru-axle.
- **Fork:** Checkpoint SL full-carbon, tapered carbon steerer, **12x100 mm** thru-axle, **45 mm** offset/rake; fender + rack + fork-cargo (3-pack) mounts.

### Drivetrain (1x12, SRAM Rival XPLR AXS — wireless electronic)
- **Shifters/controls:** SRAM Rival eTap AXS HRD (hydraulic disc, electronic)
- **Crank:** SRAM Rival, DUB, **40T**, arm length by frame size (172.5 mm on 54–58 cm)
- **Bottom bracket:** SRAM DUB, **T47 threaded**
- **Cassette:** SRAM Rival XPLR XG-1251, **10–44T, 12-speed** (XDR driver body)
- **Chain:** SRAM Rival Flattop, 12-speed
- **Rear derailleur:** SRAM Rival XPLR eTap AXS (long cage, XPLR-specific)

### Brakes
- SRAM Rival hydraulic disc, flat mount
- Rotors: **160 mm front / 160 mm rear** (SRAM Paceline / CenterLine, 6-bolt — verify bolt pattern on your rotors)

### Wheels & tires
- **Wheels:** Bontrager Paradigm Comp 25, tubeless-ready, 25 mm-wide alloy rim, 100x12 / 142x12
- **Tires:** Bontrager GR1 Team Issue, **700x40c**, tubeless-ready
- **Max tire clearance:** **700x45c** (≈ 650x53 mm on 650b)

### Cockpit & contact
- **Handlebar:** Bontrager alloy gravel bar (flared), width by size
- **Stem:** Bontrager alloy, Blendr-compatible
- **Bar tape:** Bontrager gel/cork tape
- **Saddle:** Bontrager Verse Comp
- **Seatpost:** Bontrager carbon, **27.2 mm** (rigid, stock)
- **Headset:** integrated / cartridge bearing, tapered (1-1/8" upper, 1.5" lower) — verify bearing part numbers against service manual

### Weight & limits
- ~9.3 kg / ~20.5 lb (56 cm, stock, no pedals) — 54 cm slightly lighter
- Trek weight limit (rider + cargo + bike): **125 kg / 275 lb**
