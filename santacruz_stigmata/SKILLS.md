# SKILLS.md — Santa Cruz Stigmata (race build) maintenance skills

Maintenance skill index for the gravel/CX race bike (1st-gen aluminum Stigmata, 1×10,
disc front / canti rear, tubeless). Was the commuter; now raced. See `specs.md` for the
build and open questions. Status: ☐ not done · ◐ done once · ☑ confident

---

## 1. Cantilever brake tuning (rear)
Status: ☐

- Skills: set pad height/toe-in on the rim, straddle-wire height, L/R spring-tension
  balance, straddle cable length, squeal fixes.
- Gotchas: canti feel is all in the straddle height — lower yoke = more power but less
  tire/mud clearance and more fork/stay judder risk. Toe-in ~1 mm. Kool Stop Salmon
  for wet rims. Watch pad-to-rim as the rim wears thin.
- Tools: 5 mm hex, 4th-hand cable puller, needle-nose, a matchbook/business card for toe-in.

## 2. Avid BB7 mechanical disc (front)
Status: ☐

- Skills: independent inner/outer pad-dial adjustment, rotor truing, pad swap, bed-in,
  set lever throw, chase cable friction.
- Gotchas: **confirm it's a BB7 Road** (drop-bar lever pull) — see specs.md §3. Set the
  inner (fixed-side) pad first to a light rub, then the outer. Re-dial every few mm of
  wear. Compressionless housing to the caliper makes a big difference.
- Tools: T25, 5 mm hex, rotor truing fork, isopropyl, spare pads.

## 3. SRAM 1×10 drivetrain (Force shifter + Exact-Actuation rear mech)
Status: ☐

- Skills: index a road/MTB Exact-Actuation mix, set B-tension and high/low limits for a
  12–32, chain length for a 1x (big-big + 2 links, or big-big + 4 with a clutch),
  dial out chain drops.
- Gotchas: Force shifter + SRAM **10sp** MTB or road RD = compatible (Exact Actuation);
  **11sp MTB is NOT**. 12–32 needs mid/long cage — see specs.md §2. Re-check chain
  length and B-gap any time the cassette or ring changes.
- Chain retention: no front derailleur now. If it drops the chain, fit a narrow-wide
  ring and/or a top guide (braze-on or clamp-on chain keeper on the old FD tab).
- Tools: chain tool, 10sp quick link, T25/hex, cable cutter, chain-wear gauge.

## 4. White Industries rear hub service
Status: ☐

- Skills: annual teardown — pull the freehub, clean and regrease pawls + drive ring,
  check bearing preload.
- Gotchas: **Lubriplate No. 105** grease (light — heavy grease makes pawls stick and
  skip). Never mix pawls with the wrong ratchet ring. Note pawl count (24 vs 48 pt).
- Tools: cone wrenches / hex per hub model, Lubriplate 105, rag, gloves. Get the exact
  procedure from whiteind.com for the specific hub model once identified.

## 5. Easton EA70 XC front hub service
Status: ☐

- Skills: bearing + freehub service, check for play, replace cartridge bearings.
- Gotchas: this generation of Easton hub is known for freehub wear — service at the
  first sign of notchiness or play. Service kits still available.

## 6. Mixed wheelset + tubeless care (disc front / rim rear)
Status: ☐

- Skills: tubeless mount/seat, **re-tape a converted (non-TLR) rim**, sealant refresh,
  plug/boot a race cut, monitor rear rim brake-track wear, keep the custom rear wheel
  true, record spoke specs.
- Gotchas: these are a **DIY tubeless conversion** — the rim tape is the air seal, so
  check it at every tire change and re-tape if it lifts at the valve/nipple holes.
  Only **~2 mm rear clearance** — mounted width varies with rim internal width and tire
  model, so measure before committing to a 40 mm. Rear rim also wears from canti braking
  on grit — hub outlasts rims, plan an eventual rebuild on the same hub. Front disc:
  check rotor/caliper, 6-bolt torque 6.2 Nm.
- Tools: tubeless pump/booster, valve core tool, tubeless tape (right width for the rim),
  sealant, plugs, spare tube, tire levers.

## 7. Race-day tire & clearance management
Status: ☐

- Skills: dry↔wet swap (40 mm ↔ 35 mm rear), set race pressures for tire width + course
  + rider weight, quick pre-grid tubeless check, spare-wheel/pit setup.
- Gotchas: the **rear is always the limiting end** — a 40 mm packs with mud and can lock
  the wheel; the 35 mm is the wet/mud setup. Front has room to stay at race width.
- Tools: pressure gauge, pump, pre-mounted spare tires/wheels, sealant, plugs.

## 8. Wet / muddy race recovery
Status: ☐

- Skills: post-race rinse (never blast hubs/BB), dry and inspect the rim brake track,
  re-lube chain + canti/BB7 pivots, check pad wear (grit accelerates it), top up
  tubeless sealant slung out during the race.
- Gotchas: mechanical everything — keep housing ends sealed and cables fresh; grit in a
  cable run kills shifting and braking feel fast.

## 9. Race-season frame inspection (aluminium)
Status: ☐

- Skills: check the **top-tube dent** for propagating cracks, inspect welds (head tube,
  BB, dropouts, canti bosses), check the head-tube area where the anodizing is worn for
  corrosion, verify no cracks around the removed-FD braze-on.
- Gotchas: race loads > commuter loads. Aluminium fails suddenly, not gradually — a
  crack at the dent or a weld toe ends the frame. Inspect at season start, mid-season,
  and after any crash. Seal bare alloy at the head tube.

## Recurring calendar

| Interval | Task |
|---|---|
| Every race | Tubeless check, pressures, tire choice for conditions, drivetrain + brake once-over, bolt check |
| Weekly (training) | Wipe/relube chain, brake pad + rotor check, quick true check, sealant sloshing? |
| Monthly | Rear rim wear check, cable/housing inspection, headset + hub play, chain wear gauge |
| Mid-season + post-crash | Full frame inspection (§9), cantilever + BB7 re-tune, tubeless sealant refresh |
| Annually / off-season | White Industries hub teardown/regrease, Easton front hub service, fresh cables & housing, full frame + fork inspection |
